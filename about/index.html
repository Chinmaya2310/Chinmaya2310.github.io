How Generic Bachat works

India's Jan Aushadhi scheme sells generic medicines for a fraction of brand prices, but few people know which generic replaces the brand on their prescription. This site answers that one question, honestly.

Data sources

  • Jan Aushadhi products and MRPs: the official product list published by PMBI (Pharmaceuticals & Medical Devices Bureau of India) on janaushadhi.gov.in. 2,453 products, fetched 17 November 2026.
  • Jan Aushadhi Kendras: the official store list from the same site: 21,837 Kendras with addresses, PIN codes and map coordinates.
  • Brand prices and compositions: the open Indian Medicine Dataset (MIT licence), compiled from public online-pharmacy listings. Brand MRPs change, so treat them as indicative.

How a brand is matched to a generic

  1. Each Jan Aushadhi name (for example “Pantoprazole Gastro Resistant Tablets IP 40 mg”) is parsed into its salts and strengths, and its dosage form (tablet or capsule).
  2. Salt names are normalised: “Amoxycillin” = “Amoxicillin”, and counter-ions are dropped (“Metformin Hydrochloride” = “Metformin”). Strengths are converted to the same unit (500 mcg = 0.5 mg).
  3. Sustained-release products are only matched with sustained-release products, never with normal tablets.
  4. A brand matches only if every salt and strength is identical.
  5. The brand dataset records at most two ingredients, so a three-ingredient brand could look like a two-ingredient one. To avoid that, two-ingredient brands are skipped when their name carries a marker that, in the data, almost always means a third ingredient (for example “SP”, “Trio”, “Gold”, “AMH”), when their name shows three strengths, or when the same maker sells a shorter-named brand with the same recorded composition (Telma-AM vs Telma-AM-H).
  6. Prices are compared per tablet or capsule, so different pack sizes are fair.

Result: 974 Jan Aushadhi tablets and capsules matched to 106,784 branded products. The median brand costs 70% more than its Jan Aushadhi equivalent.

Limits

  • Syrups, injections, creams and devices are not compared yet (1,479 Jan Aushadhi products), because their pack sizes are hard to compare fairly.
  • Availability varies by Kendra. Call or visit before you travel.
  • Some brands differ in coating or release type, or contain an extra ingredient, in ways the data doesn't show. The composition printed on the pack is the final word; every result tells you exactly what it should say.
Before you switch: show this page to your doctor or pharmacist. Medicines with the same salt and strength are meant to work the same way, but some (for example thyroid, epilepsy or heart-rhythm medicines) need monitoring when you change makers. Never stop or change a dose on your own.

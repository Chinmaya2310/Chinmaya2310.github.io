What is the generic name of Baclof 10?

Baclof 10 contains Baclofen 10 mg. Jan Aushadhi sells the same thing as “Baclofen Tablets IP 10 mg”.

Can I take the Jan Aushadhi version instead of Baclof 10?

It has the same salt in the same strength, so it should work the same way. Ask your doctor or pharmacist before switching.

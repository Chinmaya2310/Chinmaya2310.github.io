What is the generic name of Ciplar 10?

Ciplar 10 contains Propranolol 10 mg. Jan Aushadhi sells the same thing as “Propranolol Tablets IP 10mg”.

Can I take the Jan Aushadhi version instead of Ciplar 10?

It has the same salt in the same strength, so it should work the same way. Ask your doctor or pharmacist before switching.

Questions

What is the generic name of Dexona?

Dexona contains Dexamethasone 500 mcg. The Jan Aushadhi product with the same composition is “Dexamethasone Tablets IP 0.5 mg”.

What is the cheaper substitute for Dexona?

The Jan Aushadhi generic Dexamethasone 500 mcg tablet costs ₹0.19 per tablet vs. ₹0.23 for Dexona, a saving of about 20%.

Can I replace Dexona with the Jan Aushadhi generic?

Medicines with the same salt, strength and dosage form are meant to be interchangeable, but ask your doctor or pharmacist before switching.

Before you switch: show this page to your doctor or pharmacist. Medicines with the same salt and strength are meant to work the same way, but some (for example thyroid, epilepsy or heart-rhythm medicines) need monitoring when you change makers. Never stop or change a dose on your own.

What is the generic name of Geminor M 1 PR?

Geminor M 1 PR contains Glimepiride 1 mg + Metformin 500 mg (SR). Jan Aushadhi sells the same thing as “Metformin Hydrochloride 500mg (Prolonged release) and Glimepiride 1mg Tablets IP”.

Can I take the Jan Aushadhi version instead of Geminor M 1 PR?

It has the same salt in the same strength, so it should work the same way. Ask your doctor or pharmacist before switching.

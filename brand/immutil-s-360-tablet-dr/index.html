What is the generic name of Immutil-S 360 DR?

Immutil-S 360 DR contains Mycophenolate 360 mg. Jan Aushadhi sells the same thing as “Mycophenolate Sodium Gastro-resistant Tablets 360mg”.

Can I take the Jan Aushadhi version instead of Immutil-S 360 DR?

It has the same salt in the same strength, so it should work the same way. Ask your doctor or pharmacist before switching.

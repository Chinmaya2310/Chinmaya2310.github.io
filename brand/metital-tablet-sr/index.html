Questions

What is the generic name of Metital SR?

Metital SR contains Metformin 500 mg + Myo Inositol 600 mg (SR). The Jan Aushadhi product with the same composition is “Metformin Hydrochloride (sustained-relase) 500mg and Myo-Inositol 600mg Tablets”.

What is the cheaper substitute for Metital SR?

The Jan Aushadhi generic Metformin 500 mg + Myo Inositol 600 mg (SR) tablet costs ₹8.25 per tablet vs. ₹25.77 for Metital SR, a saving of about 67%.

Can I replace Metital SR with the Jan Aushadhi generic?

Medicines with the same salt, strength and dosage form are meant to be interchangeable, but ask your doctor or pharmacist before switching.

Before you switch: show this page to your doctor or pharmacist. Medicines with the same salt and strength are meant to work the same way, but some (for example thyroid, epilepsy or heart-rhythm medicines) need monitoring when you change makers. Never stop or change a dose on your own.

What is the generic name of Montecip LC?

Montecip LC contains Levocetirizine 5 mg + Montelukast 10 mg. Jan Aushadhi sells the same thing as “Montelukast Sodium 10mg and Levocetirizine 5mg Tablets IP”.

Can I take the Jan Aushadhi version instead of Montecip LC?

It has the same salt in the same strength, so it should work the same way. Ask your doctor or pharmacist before switching.

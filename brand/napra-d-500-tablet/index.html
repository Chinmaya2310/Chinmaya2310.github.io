Questions

What is the generic name of Napra D 500?

Napra D 500 contains Domperidone 10 mg + Naproxen 500 mg. The Jan Aushadhi product with the same composition is “Naproxen 500 mg and Domperidone 10 mg Tablet”.

What is the cheaper substitute for Napra D 500?

The Jan Aushadhi generic Domperidone 10 mg + Naproxen 500 mg tablet costs ₹4.88 per tablet vs. ₹9.80 for Napra D 500, a saving of about 50%.

Can I replace Napra D 500 with the Jan Aushadhi generic?

Medicines with the same salt, strength and dosage form are meant to be interchangeable, but ask your doctor or pharmacist before switching.

Before you switch: show this page to your doctor or pharmacist. Medicines with the same salt and strength are meant to work the same way, but some (for example thyroid, epilepsy or heart-rhythm medicines) need monitoring when you change makers. Never stop or change a dose on your own.

What is the generic name of Prax 10?

Prax 10 contains Prasugrel 10 mg. Jan Aushadhi sells the same thing as “Prasugrel Tablets IP 10 mg”.

Can I take the Jan Aushadhi version instead of Prax 10?

It has the same salt in the same strength, so it should work the same way. Ask your doctor or pharmacist before switching.

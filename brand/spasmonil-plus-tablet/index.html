What is the generic name of Spasmonil Plus?

Spasmonil Plus contains Dicyclomine 10 mg + Mefenamic Acid 250 mg. Jan Aushadhi sells the same thing as “Dicyclomine Hydrochloride 10mg and Mefenamic Acid 250mg Tablets IP”.

Can I take the Jan Aushadhi version instead of Spasmonil Plus?

It has the same salt in the same strength, so it should work the same way. Ask your doctor or pharmacist before switching.

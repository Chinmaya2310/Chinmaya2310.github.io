What is the generic name of Tenvir?

Tenvir contains Tenofovir Disoproxil 300 mg. Jan Aushadhi sells the same thing as “Tenofovir Disoproxil Fumarate Tablets IP 300 mg”.

Can I take the Jan Aushadhi version instead of Tenvir?

It has the same salt in the same strength, so it should work the same way. Ask your doctor or pharmacist before switching.

Same salt and strength means the medicines should work the same way, but please check with your doctor or pharmacist first. Thyroid, epilepsy and heart-rhythm medicines in particular may need a check-up after you change brands.

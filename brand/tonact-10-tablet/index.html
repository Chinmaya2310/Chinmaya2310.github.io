What is the generic name of Tonact 10?

Tonact 10 contains Atorvastatin 10 mg. Jan Aushadhi sells the same thing as “Atorvastatin Tablets IP 10mg”.

Can I take the Jan Aushadhi version instead of Tonact 10?

It has the same salt in the same strength, so it should work the same way. Ask your doctor or pharmacist before switching.

What is the generic name of Veltam Plus MR?

Veltam Plus MR contains Dutasteride 500 mcg + Tamsulosin 400 mcg (SR). Jan Aushadhi sells the same thing as “Tamsulosin Hydrochloride (Modified Release) 0.4mg and Dutasteride 0.5mg Tablets”.

Can I take the Jan Aushadhi version instead of Veltam Plus MR?

It has the same salt in the same strength, so it should work the same way. Ask your doctor or pharmacist before switching.

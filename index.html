    Frequently asked questions

    What is a Jan Aushadhi medicine?

    Jan Aushadhi medicines are unbranded generic medicines sold through Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Kendras, a Government of India scheme run by PMBI. They contain the same active ingredients (salts) as branded medicines, usually at a much lower price.

    Are generic medicines as good as branded ones?

    In India, a generic and a branded medicine with the same salt, strength and dosage form are meant to be therapeutically equivalent, and Jan Aushadhi products are tested by NABL-accredited labs. Your doctor or pharmacist can confirm whether switching is right for you.

    How does Generic Bachat find the generic?

    We match the salt (active ingredient), its strength and the dosage form, such as 650 mg paracetamol tablet, between the brand and the official Jan Aushadhi product list, and then compare the price per tablet or capsule.

    Why might the price at the shop be different?

    Jan Aushadhi prices come from the official PMBI product list. Brand prices come from a public dataset of online pharmacy listings, so a brand's current MRP may be higher or lower. Always check the MRP printed on the strip.

    Where can I buy Jan Aushadhi medicines?

    At any of the 21,000+ Jan Aushadhi Kendras across India. Use our store finder to see the nearest ones on a map.

    Before you switch: show this page to your doctor or pharmacist. Medicines with the same salt and strength are meant to work the same way, but some (for example thyroid, epilepsy or heart-rhythm medicines) need monitoring when you change makers. Never stop or change a dose on your own.
